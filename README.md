RXLab Physio Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
RXLab Physio is a private physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation practice operating within Gainline Strength in East Kilbride, Scotland. The business combines clinical physiotherapy expertise with strength training and personalised rehabilitation to help athletes and individuals recover from injuries, improve physical performance and return to their activities.

The redesigned homepage positions RXLab as a specialist, performance-focused physiotherapy provider. It highlights its expertise in CrossFit, Ironman and sports rehabilitation while making treatment options, practitioner credentials and appointment booking easier to understand.
2. Main Improvements
1. Stronger Specialist Positioning
- Strengthened the homepage messaging around CrossFit, Ironman and sports injury rehabilitation.
- Highlighted the combination of clinical physiotherapy and strength-based rehabilitation.
- Communicated RXLab's specialist approach more clearly to athletes and active individuals.
Problem addressed: The previous homepage did not sufficiently highlight RXLab's specialist expertise or distinguish it from general physiotherapy providers.
2. Clearer Services and Pricing Journey
- Organised physiotherapy consultations, returning appointments, massage and rehabilitation services into clearer categories.
- Improved the presentation of available appointment options and pricing information.
- Made the online booking pathway easier to identify.
Problem addressed: The previous service presentation made it difficult for visitors to compare treatment options, understand pricing and decide which appointment to book.
3. Stronger Practitioner and Clinic Credibility
- Improved the presentation of Lauren's and Gregor's qualifications and clinical experience.
- Highlighted their backgrounds in physiotherapy, sports rehabilitation and musculoskeletal care.
- Connected RXLab's treatment approach with its operating environment at Gainline Strength.
Problem addressed: Practitioner expertise and the strength-training environment were not sufficiently integrated into the homepage's trust-building experience.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Established a professional, energetic and performance-focused appearance reflecting RXLab's sports rehabilitation positioning.
- Homepage Layout: Improved content hierarchy to give specialist services, practitioners and booking information greater prominence.
- User Experience: Simplified service discovery and made appointment options easier to understand.
- Content Consistency: Presented physiotherapy, massage and rehabilitation services within a more structured format.
- Trust and Credibility: Strengthened the visibility of practitioner qualifications, clinical experience and the Gainline Strength environment.

Project Focus: Specialist sports rehabilitation positioning, clearer treatment and pricing information, and stronger practitioner credibility.
