A different approach

Physiotherapy that goes beyond pain relief.

Getting out of pain is important. Getting back to the things that make you feel like yourself is just as important.

At RXLab, rehabilitation is built around your individual needs. Whether you are returning to competitive sport, rebuilding strength after an injury or managing persistent discomfort, your treatment should reflect your real-life goals.

Clinical assessment, personalised treatment and practical movement-based rehabilitation come together to help you work towards meaningful progress.

Your recovery should prepare you for life beyond the treatment room.
What we do

Find the support that fits your goals.

Explore RXLab's physiotherapy, massage and rehabilitation services. Choose a starting point that reflects what you need.

Physiotherapy

Initial assessments and returning appointments to understand your injury, explore treatment options and support your recovery.

Sports Massage

Targeted soft-tissue treatment to address muscle tension, support recovery and help you stay comfortable and active.

Strength-Based Rehab

Individualised, physio-led rehabilitation and strength training designed around your capabilities and activity goals.

Full Body MOT

A longer assessment for complex or multiple injuries, incorporating objective testing and treatment tailored to your needs.

Online Rehabilitation

Remote rehabilitation with video analysis, regular check-ins and progress reviews for people who need flexible support.

Sports Injury Rehab

Rehabilitation informed by the demands of sport, including experience with CrossFit, Ironman, rugby and running-related injuries.

Meet your practitioners

Clinical experience. A personal approach.

Get to know the practitioners behind RXLab and the experience they bring to physiotherapy and rehabilitation.

01

Lauren

BSc Physiology and Sports Science · Master's in Physiotherapy

Lauren graduated from the University of Glasgow with a degree in Physiology and Sports Science before completing her Master's in Physiotherapy.

Her experience includes NHS community work, private practice and working with football and rugby teams.

As a CrossFit competitor and Olympic weightlifting enthusiast, Lauren brings an understanding of the physical demands of sport to her rehabilitation approach.

02

Gregor

First Class BSc (Hons) Physiotherapy · 2023

Gregor graduated from Queen Margaret University and has developed clinical experience across NHS acute and community settings.

His background includes musculoskeletal care, orthopaedics, neurological rehabilitation, paediatrics and community physiotherapy.

A keen triathlete and former mountain bike coach, Gregor has a particular interest in sports injuries, running rehabilitation and helping people return to the activities they enjoy.
